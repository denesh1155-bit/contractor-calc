Privacy policy

“Georgian Tales” is an app for children. We collect no personal data. The child's name, avatar, stickers, coloured pictures and any parent voice recordings are stored only on the device. The microphone is used only when a parent chooses to record a tale. Subscriptions are processed by Google Play; our server only checks whether a subscription is active.
