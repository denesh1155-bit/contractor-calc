Essential Knowledge

Markup vs. Margin:
The Difference That Could Cost You Thousands

Most contractors lose money because they confuse these two. Here's the definitive explanation.

+%

Markup

Profit expressed as a percentage of your cost.

Markup % = (Profit ÷ Cost) × 100
Example: Cost = $1,000 | Markup = 25%
Price = $1,000 × 1.25 = $1,250
Profit = $250
Used by: Suppliers, Wholesalers
≠
%₍price₎

Profit Margin

Profit expressed as a percentage of the selling price.

Margin % = (Profit ÷ Price) × 100
Example: Cost = $1,000 | Margin = 20%
Price = $1,000 ÷ (1 − 0.20) = $1,250
Profit = $250 (= 20% of $1,250)
Used by: Contractors, Accountants
⚠️
The Common Mistake: If you quote a 20% markup but expect a 20% margin — you're undercharging by ~5%. A 25% markup gives you only a 20% margin. They are never equal.
